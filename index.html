Mission 1 · Ethics vs Morals

Before we judge AI, we need to know what "right" and "wrong" mean. People use two words for this: ethics and morals.

Compare them

EthicsMorals
DefinitionRules from society or a professionPersonal beliefs of right and wrong
SourceExternal (outside you)Internal (inside you)
ExampleLegal ethics, medical ethicsReligious teachings, personal values (honesty, respect, kindness)
FocusSociety's standardsYour conscience: your own sense of right and wrong
FlexibilityMore universal and consistentVaries from person to person
💡 Memory trick: Ethics comes from External rules. Morals come from Me.
Ethics asks: "Is it good to speak the truth in all situations?" · Morals says: "I always speak the truth."

Sort it! Ethics or Morals?

Read each statement. Is it a rule from society or a profession, or a person's own belief?
